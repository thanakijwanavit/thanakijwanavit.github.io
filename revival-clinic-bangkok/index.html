How the room is run

Medicine first.
Then the drip.

  1. 01

    A doctor prepares every drip

    IV work is screened, mixed, and given by a physician. The clinic does not hand infusions to sales staff.

  2. 02

    Imported, preservative-free formulas

    Vitamin drips are mixed from products brought in from Japan and Switzerland, not off-the-shelf local cocktails.

  3. 03

    English at the chair

    Doctors and staff work in English as well as Thai. International patients are the everyday caseload, not an afterthought.

  4. 04

    The price on the list is the price

    No hidden add-ons, no hard sell, no commission agents on the floor. What is published is what is charged.
