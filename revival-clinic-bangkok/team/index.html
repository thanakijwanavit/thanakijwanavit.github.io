The room

Licensed people at every step.

Nursing is done by licensed nurses. Medical decisions stay with qualified doctors. The clinic does not use non-medical staff to cut the cost of care.

Co-founder

Dr. Chontirot

Dermatology, nutritional IV therapy, chelation, hair restoration

Dr. Chontirot co-founded Revival Clinic and still takes IV cases from history-taking through mixing and administration. After medical school she trained in dermatology at the Skin Institute, then in primary-care skin cancer medicine at the University of Queensland. A hair-transplant fellowship and a preventive-medicine board certification followed. She holds certification in nutritional IV therapy and chelation from The American Academy of Anti-Aging Medicine, and later studied genetics and genomics at Stanford.

  • Dermatology training, Skin Institute
  • Primary-care skin cancer medicine, University of Queensland
  • Hair-transplant fellowship
  • Board certification in preventive medicine
  • Nutritional IV therapy and chelation, A4M
  • Genetics and genomics, Stanford University

Physician

Dr. Panapon Lumdee

Aesthetic medicine, hair restoration, IV therapy, nutrition

Dr. Panapon Lumdee works across aesthetic medicine and hair restoration, and often pairs those procedures with IV and nutrition protocols. She holds qualifications from the American Naturopathic Certification Board in Roseville, California, and completed further training at the Institute of Lifestyle Medicine at Harvard Medical School. In Bangkok she studied precision nutrition and wellness at the Integrative Functional Nutrition and Wellness Center. She has trained with the Association of Thai Aesthetic Physicians and at IMCAS Asia.

  • American Naturopathic Certification Board, Roseville, CA
  • Institute of Lifestyle Medicine, Harvard Medical School
  • Precision Nutrition and Wellness, Bangkok
  • Association of Thai Aesthetic Physicians
  • IMCAS Asia programmes

Aesthetic and anti-aging specialist

Dr. Phattarakarn

Botox, fillers, thread lifting, lasers, regenerative medicine

Dr. Phattarakarn is board-certified in anti-aging and regenerative medicine by the American Board of Anti-Aging and Regenerative Medicine (ABAARM). Her clinical work covers botulinum toxin, fillers, thread lifting, and a range of laser treatments. Cadaver-based anatomy courses sit behind the injection work; she continues to attend Thai and international aesthetic meetings to keep technique current.

  • ABAARM board certification
  • Advanced Botox, filler, and thread-lift training
  • Laser treatments across common aesthetic indications
  • Cadaver-based facial anatomy courses
